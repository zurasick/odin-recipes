Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients

paghetti Bolognese is a popular pasta dish made with spaghetti and a rich meat and tomato sauce.

  • Spaghetti
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chopped onion and garlic.
  3. Add the minced beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Serve the sauce over the cooked spaghetti.
