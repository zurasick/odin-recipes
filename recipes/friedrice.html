Fried Rice

Fried rice is a popular dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with various ingredients such as vegetables, eggs, and meat or seafood. It is often seasoned with soy sauce, garlic, and other spices to enhance its flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, bell peppers)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add garlic and onion, and stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook for another 2-3 minutes until tender.
  4. Push the vegetables to the side of the skillet and pour the beaten eggs into the center. Scramble the eggs until fully cooked.
  5. Add the cooked rice to the skillet and stir to combine with the vegetables and eggs.
  6. Season with soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Stir well to distribute the seasonings evenly.
  7. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes until everything is heated through and well combined.
  8. Remove from heat and serve hot.
